## Task 2: Understanding Project Context

Before starting a project, these are five important things to understand:

### 1. What is the goal of the project?

Understanding the purpose, expected outcome and success criteria ensures the development team is working towards the correct objective.

### 2. Who are the stakeholders and users?

Identifying the people involved helps understand their requirements, responsibilities and expectations.

### 3. What are the project requirements?

Understanding required features, technology choices, limitations and business requirements helps guide development decisions.

### 4. What resources are available?

Knowing available tools, budget, team members, time and technology helps create a realistic project plan.

### 5. What risks and challenges exist?

Identifying possible technical, security and business risks early helps the team prepare solutions and reduce problems during development.
